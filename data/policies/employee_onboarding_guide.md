# Employee Onboarding Guide

**Document ID:** POL-005
**Version:** 1.4
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-15
**Status:** Active

## Section 1 — Purpose and Scope
This guide outlines the steps and timelines for new employee onboarding, from offer acceptance through the end of the probation period. It is intended for use by new hires, their managers, and HR business partners coordinating the process.

## Section 2 — Pre-Joining
2.1 New hires receive a welcome email with joining instructions no later than 5 working days before their start date, including IT setup steps and a first-day agenda.
2.2 Background verification must be completed before the start date; if incomplete, joining may be deferred by up to 15 days. HR will communicate any deferral to the new hire at least 3 working days before the original start date.
2.3 New hires are asked to complete a pre-joining information form (emergency contact, bank details, ID documents) at least 3 working days before their start date to avoid Day One delays.

## Section 3 — Day One
3.1 New hires must complete ID badge issuance, laptop handover, and IT account activation on their first day, coordinated by the assigned HR representative.
3.2 A buddy is assigned to every new hire for the first 30 days to assist with onboarding questions, informal team introductions, and navigating internal systems.
3.3 Managers are expected to hold a first-day 1:1 with the new hire covering role expectations, immediate priorities, and an introduction to the team's working norms.

## Section 4 — Mandatory Training
4.1 New hires must complete Information Security Awareness training within the first 7 working days, covering password hygiene, phishing recognition, and data classification basics from the Information Security Policy.
4.2 New hires must complete Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment training within the first 30 days, delivered through the Learning Management System.
4.3 Failure to complete mandatory training within the specified window results in automatic escalation to the employee's manager and HR, and repeated non-completion may result in restricted system access until training is finished.
4.4 Role-specific compliance training (e.g., for Finance, Legal, or client-facing roles) is assigned separately by the relevant department and is not covered by this general guide.

## Section 5 — Probation Period
5.1 The standard probation period is 90 days from the date of joining, during which performance and cultural fit are assessed against role expectations shared at Day One.
5.2 Probation may be extended by up to 30 days at the manager's discretion if performance expectations are not yet met, with written notice to the employee explaining the reason and revised expectations.
5.3 A formal probation review meeting must be held between the manager and employee before the 90-day mark, with the outcome (confirmation, extension, or non-confirmation) documented in the HR system.

## Section 6 — Documentation
6.1 New hires must submit signed copies of the Employment Agreement, NDA, and Code of Conduct acknowledgment within the first week, uploaded through the HR portal.
6.2 Bank details for payroll must be submitted before the first payroll cycle to avoid delayed payment; late submission may push the first payment to the following cycle.
6.3 Employees changing bank details after joining must update them through the HR portal at least 5 working days before the next payroll run.

## Section 7 — Equipment
7.1 Standard equipment (laptop, headset, access card) is issued on Day One. Additional equipment requests must go through the IT Request Portal and are subject to role-based approval.
7.2 Equipment for remote or hybrid new hires is shipped in advance of the start date where the new hire's designated work location is more than 50km from the nearest office, coordinated with the IT logistics team.
