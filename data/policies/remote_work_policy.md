# Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** POL-003
**Version:** 3.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-03-15
**Status:** Active

## Section 1 — Purpose and Scope
This policy sets expectations for employees working outside a company office, covering hybrid arrangements, fully remote work, and cross-border remote work. It applies to all full-time employees; interns and fixed-term contractors require explicit written approval from HR to work remotely under any arrangement.

## Section 2 — Eligibility
2.1 Employees who have completed 3 months of continuous service are eligible to apply for a hybrid work arrangement, submitted through the HR portal with manager co-sign-off.
2.2 New hires within their first 3 months must work from the office unless the role is designated fully remote at time of hiring, in which case the offer letter will state this explicitly.
2.3 Eligibility may be paused for employees on a Performance Improvement Plan, at the manager's discretion, until performance concerns are resolved.

## Section 3 — Hybrid Arrangement
3.1 Standard hybrid arrangement permits up to 2 remote days per week, to be agreed upon with the immediate manager and documented in the team's working pattern log.
3.2 Teams with client-facing or on-site infrastructure responsibilities may have reduced remote-day allowances, as defined by the department head; any such restriction must be communicated to affected employees in writing.
3.3 Remote days may be swapped within the same week with manager approval (e.g., swapping a Tuesday for a Thursday) but do not carry over into the following week.

## Section 4 — Fully Remote Work
4.1 Fully remote arrangements (beyond 2 days/week) require Director-level approval and a documented business justification, reviewed every 6 months.
4.2 Fully remote employees must be reachable during core hours (10:00–16:00 local office time) unless otherwise agreed, and are expected to attend at least one in-person team gathering per quarter where feasible.
4.3 Fully remote status does not change an employee's designated work location for payroll, tax, or benefits purposes unless a separate relocation process has been completed.

## Section 5 — Working From Another Country
5.1 Working remotely from a country other than the employee's registered work location requires prior approval from HR and Legal due to tax and compliance implications, and must be requested at least 15 working days in advance.
5.2 Such arrangements are capped at 20 working days per calendar year unless a separate international remote work agreement is signed. This cap resets each calendar year and does not accrue or carry forward.
5.3 Exception: This cap does not apply to employees on an approved international assignment, who are instead governed by the terms of their assignment letter.
5.4 Employees must not work from a country under active company travel restriction or sanctions watch, as published by the Legal team, regardless of remote work eligibility otherwise.

## Section 6 — Equipment and Security
6.1 The company provides a laptop and standard peripherals for remote work; home internet and furniture are the employee's responsibility unless a stipend is separately approved for the role or location.
6.2 Remote employees must connect to company systems only via the corporate VPN and must not use public, unsecured Wi-Fi for work involving confidential data, per the Information Security Policy.
6.3 Loss or damage of company equipment while working remotely must be reported to IT within 24 hours, consistent with incident reporting timelines in the Information Security Policy.

## Section 7 — Performance and Availability
7.1 Remote work privileges may be revoked by a manager if attendance in required meetings or delivery timelines are not consistently met, following at least one documented coaching conversation.
7.2 Repeated unavailability during core hours without prior notice may result in the employee being reverted to a full in-office schedule for a probationary review period of 60 days.
