# Leave Policy

**Document ID:** POL-001
**Version:** 2.3
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Status:** Active

## Section 1 — Purpose and Scope
This policy defines the types of leave available to employees, eligibility conditions, accrual mechanics, and the approval process. It applies to all full-time employees on the company payroll, including those on hybrid or remote arrangements under the Remote Work Policy. Interns and contractors are governed by separate terms specified in their engagement letters and are not covered by this policy unless explicitly stated.

## Section 2 — Annual Leave
2.1 Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of annual leave per completed month of service, up to a maximum of 21 days per calendar year. Accrual begins from the first full month of employment; the joining month is prorated based on the number of remaining working days.
2.2 Annual leave must be requested through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance. Requests submitted with less notice may still be approved at the manager's discretion but are not guaranteed, particularly during peak project periods.
2.3 Unused annual leave may be carried forward, but a maximum of 5 days may be carried into the next calendar year. Any balance beyond 5 days is forfeited as of December 31st unless the employee's manager grants a written exception (see Section 2.4). Forfeited leave is not converted to a cash payout.
2.4 Exception: Employees on active client-critical projects, as designated by their reporting manager in writing, may carry forward up to 10 days with HR approval. This exception must be requested before December 1st and re-confirmed annually; it does not automatically renew from one year to the next.
2.5 Leave taken during a public holiday that falls within an approved leave period is not deducted from the employee's leave balance; the system automatically excludes gazetted holidays from the count.

## Section 3 — Sick Leave
3.1 Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year, credited in full at the start of the year rather than accrued monthly.
3.2 Sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate submitted within 3 working days of return. A single-day sick leave does not require documentation but repeated single-day absences (more than 4 in a rolling 3-month period) may trigger a manager check-in.
3.3 Sick leave does not carry forward to the next calendar year and has no cash-equivalent payout on exit.
3.4 Sick leave taken while on approved annual leave converts the affected days back to sick leave, provided a medical certificate is furnished; the annual leave balance is restored accordingly.

## Section 4 — Parental Leave
4.1 Primary caregivers are entitled to 90 calendar days of paid parental leave, inclusive of weekends and public holidays falling within the period.
4.2 Secondary caregivers are entitled to 14 calendar days of paid parental leave, which may be split into two blocks within 6 months of the birth or adoption date, subject to manager agreement on timing.
4.3 Parental leave must be requested at least 30 days before the expected start date, except in cases of premature birth or medical emergency, where retroactive approval within 7 days of the leave starting is permitted.
4.4 Adoptive parents are entitled to the same parental leave terms as biological parents, effective from the date of legal custody transfer.

## Section 5 — Unpaid Leave
5.1 Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be requested for personal reasons, subject to manager and HR approval. Approval is not guaranteed and depends on business need and prior leave usage patterns.
5.2 Unpaid leave exceeding 30 days requires Director-level approval and may affect benefit continuity, including health insurance coverage, which is suspended after 45 consecutive days of unpaid leave unless the employee opts to self-fund premiums during that period.

## Section 6 — Emergency Leave
6.1 Employees may take up to 3 days of emergency leave per year for family emergencies, bereavement, or unforeseen circumstances, without advance notice, subject to notifying the manager within 24 hours by any reasonable means (call, message, or email).
6.2 Emergency leave beyond 3 days in a year is deducted from the annual leave balance, or treated as unpaid leave if the annual balance is exhausted.

## Section 7 — Leave During Notice Period
7.1 Annual leave may not be availed during the final 15 days of a resignation notice period unless mutually agreed upon with the manager in writing, to allow for adequate handover.
7.2 Any unused annual leave balance at the time of exit, up to the standard cap in Section 2.1, is paid out as part of the final settlement, calculated on the employee's last drawn basic salary.
