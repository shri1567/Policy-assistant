# Acceptable Use Policy

**Document ID:** POL-006
**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** 2025-09-01
**Status:** Active

## Section 1 — Purpose and Scope
This policy defines acceptable use of company IT systems, email, and internet access by all employees, contractors, and interns using company-owned or company-managed devices and accounts.

## Section 2 — General Use
2.1 Company systems are provided primarily for business purposes. Limited, reasonable personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties, consume excessive bandwidth, or violate other company policies.
2.2 Employees must not use company systems to access, store, or distribute illegal, offensive, or discriminatory content, including content that violates the company's Anti-Harassment policy.
2.3 Personal use of company systems for outside business activities (e.g., running a personal side business) is not permitted at any time.

## Section 3 — Email and Communication
3.1 Company email must not be used to send Confidential or Restricted data to personal or unauthorized external email addresses, consistent with the data handling rules in the Information Security Policy.
3.2 Mass emails to all-staff distribution lists require approval from the Communications team, submitted at least 2 working days in advance except for urgent operational announcements.
3.3 Auto-forwarding of company email to personal or external accounts is disabled by default and may only be enabled with a documented business justification approved by IT Security.

## Section 4 — Software and Installations
4.1 Employees must not install unlicensed or unapproved software on company devices, including free or open-source tools not already on the approved software list.
4.2 Software installation requests must go through IT and be limited to tools relevant to the employee's role; requests are typically reviewed within 3 working days.
4.3 Browser extensions that request access to browsing data or credentials require IT approval before installation on company devices.

## Section 5 — Internet Usage
5.1 Access to peer-to-peer file sharing, torrenting, and unauthorized proxy/VPN services on company networks is prohibited at all times.
5.2 Reasonable personal browsing during breaks is permitted; excessive personal use affecting productivity may be addressed by the manager as a performance matter rather than a policy violation in most cases.
5.3 Streaming of high-bandwidth personal content (video, music) during work hours should be limited, particularly when connected via VPN, to avoid degrading network performance for business-critical applications.

## Section 6 — Social Media
6.1 Employees must not represent official company positions on personal social media accounts without authorization from the Communications team.
6.2 Sharing Confidential or Restricted company information on any public platform is strictly prohibited, including screenshots of internal tools, dashboards, or client materials.
6.3 Employees are encouraged to add a standard disclaimer ("views are my own") when discussing industry topics related to their role on personal social media.

## Section 7 — Monitoring
7.1 The company reserves the right to monitor use of company-owned systems and networks for security and compliance purposes, in accordance with applicable local law.
7.2 Monitoring data is retained for a maximum of 12 months except where required for an active investigation, and access to monitoring data is restricted to authorized Security and HR personnel.

## Section 8 — Violations
8.1 Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, depending on severity, as determined by HR and Legal.
8.2 First-time minor violations (e.g., installing a low-risk unapproved tool) typically result in a documented warning and required remediation rather than immediate escalation, unless combined with a data security breach.
