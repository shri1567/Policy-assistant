# Travel and Expense Policy

**Document ID:** POL-002
**Version:** 1.8
**Effective Date:** 2025-11-01
**Status:** Active

## Section 1 — Purpose and Scope
This policy governs business travel booking, reimbursable expenses, and approval thresholds for all employees traveling on company business, whether domestic or international. It does not cover relocation expenses, which are governed under a separate Relocation Policy.

## Section 2 — Travel Booking
2.1 All domestic and international travel must be booked through the approved corporate travel portal, which provides negotiated rates and ensures travel insurance is automatically applied.
2.2 Economy class is the default for flights under 6 hours. Business class is permitted for flights exceeding 6 hours, subject to manager approval, and should be booked at least 10 days in advance where possible to control cost.
2.3 Exception: Employees with a documented medical condition may be approved for business class regardless of flight duration, with HR and medical documentation on file. This exception is reviewed annually and does not require re-approval for each trip once on file.
2.4 Same-day return trips under 3 hours' flight time do not require advance manager sign-off but must still be logged in the travel portal for insurance and duty-of-care purposes.

## Section 3 — Accommodation
3.1 Hotel bookings must not exceed the city-tier cap published annually by Finance (Tier 1 cities: USD 220/night; Tier 2: USD 150/night; Tier 3: USD 100/night). The current city-tier list is maintained on the Finance intranet page and updated each January.
3.2 Bookings exceeding the cap require pre-approval from the department head, with a brief justification (e.g., conference venue pricing, safety considerations in the destination city).
3.3 Extended stays for project reasons (beyond 14 consecutive nights) may qualify for a long-stay corporate rate; employees should contact the Travel Desk directly rather than booking through the standard portal.

## Section 4 — Meals and Incidentals
4.1 A daily per-diem of USD 60 is provided for international travel and USD 35 for domestic travel, covering meals and incidentals. Per-diem is paid regardless of actual spend and does not require receipts.
4.2 Per-diem is not provided for days where meals are included in a conference or client event; the per-diem for that day is reduced by 50% if only one meal is covered, and to zero if two or more meals are covered.
4.3 The day of departure and the day of return are each paid at 75% of the standard per-diem rate to reflect partial-day travel.

## Section 5 — Ground Transportation
5.1 Ride-hailing and taxi expenses are reimbursable with itemized receipts, including airport transfers and client-site travel.
5.2 Rental cars require pre-approval and are limited to mid-size vehicles unless client-facing requirements dictate otherwise (e.g., transporting equipment or multiple colleagues).
5.3 Personal vehicle use for business travel is reimbursed at the company's published per-kilometer rate, reviewed annually, and does not include fuel receipts separately.

## Section 6 — Expense Submission
6.1 Expense reports must be submitted within 14 calendar days of trip completion, through the expense management system with scanned receipts attached.
6.2 Reports submitted after 30 days will not be reimbursed except with Finance Director exception approval, which must include a written reason for the delay.
6.3 All expenses over USD 25 require an itemized receipt; expenses under USD 25 may use a self-declared entry, capped at USD 100 in self-declared entries per trip.
6.4 Expenses submitted in a foreign currency are converted using the exchange rate on the date of transaction, as sourced automatically by the expense system.

## Section 7 — Non-Reimbursable Items
7.1 The following are not reimbursable under any circumstance: personal entertainment, alcohol not part of a client meal, traffic or parking fines, spa or minibar charges, and travel insurance already covered by the corporate policy.
7.2 Alcohol as part of a client meal is reimbursable up to a reasonable limit (generally interpreted as no more than 2 drinks per attendee) and must be itemized separately from food on the receipt where possible.

## Section 8 — International Travel Requirements
8.1 Employees traveling internationally for business must confirm visa and vaccination requirements with the Travel Desk at least 21 days prior to departure.
8.2 Visa application fees and related administrative costs for business travel are reimbursable; visas required for personal travel extensions attached to a business trip are not.
